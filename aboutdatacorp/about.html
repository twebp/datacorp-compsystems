About DataCorp Computer Systems

Local Service. Decades of Experience.

DataCorp Computer Systems began when its owner moved from England to Valdosta and built a computer business of his own. Since the 1990s, DataCorp has grown alongside the community, helping generations of customers keep up with an ever-changing world of technology.

And while computers have changed dramatically over the years, DataCorp has remained refreshingly personal.

The owner still answers the phone, welcomes customers into the shop and works directly with people to understand what they need. Whether you're troubleshooting an older computer, upgrading your current system or planning a completely new build, you'll find decades of hands-on knowledge behind every recommendation.

Experience That Keeps Up With Technology

More than 40 years in the computer field doesn't mean being stuck in the past.

DataCorp continues to work with modern processors, components, custom computer builds and today's hardware and software. That experience makes it easier to look at a system, understand how its pieces work together and help customers find solutions that make sense for them.

More Than a Computer Store

Some DataCorp customers have been with the business for years, and the shop continues to support computers and systems sold throughout its history.

That kind of relationship is what DataCorp has always been about: not simply selling a computer or completing a repair, but being a local place people can come back to when they need help.

Whether you're a longtime customer or walking through the door for the first time, you're always welcome at DataCorp.
